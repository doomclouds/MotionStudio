# What to retain and how to reuse it

1. **Asymmetry does the work.** A small section mark owns the upper left; the saturated card occupies the right while the left middle remains empty. Do not “balance” it by adding another card. Its square right edge is intentional contact with the full frame, not missing content.
2. **One dominant color, two scales of type.** Archivo Black provides the statement and section number; much smaller tracked Space Grotesk carries metadata. Do not introduce another display family. The label belongs to the card but is not a third headline.
3. **Bottom-align the content group, not each line.** The card leaves substantial quiet space above the launch label. Preserve96px bottom padding and122.2px baseline spacing. Author two short lines; resolve long copy editorially instead of silently shrinking it.
4. **The shadow is a colored atmosphere.** Keep it softer than the card edge and on the left/bottom. Native correction preserves the intent but not exact browser blur. Do not turn it into a hard black drop shadow.
5. **Navigation is orientation, not UI here.** Four uppercase stages, one colored active stage, subdued remainder. Section number supports sequence. This static study has no click state or progress transition.
6. **Repeat within a coherent chapter family.** Transfer changes section01→02, active stage0→1, orange→pale mint, title and footer; it calls the same recipe. Component helper exports the card with its text hierarchy for a caller-owned composition. Neither demonstration is an extra reference work.
7. **Keep scope honest.** “LAUNCH · 2026” is fictional original template copy. Transfer footer visibly says “FICTIONAL DEMO · NO PRODUCT LAUNCH”. Do not present either as evidence that a product shipped or that the user approved the aesthetic.
