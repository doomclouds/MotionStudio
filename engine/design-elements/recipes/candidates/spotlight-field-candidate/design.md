# Make the moving light explain the discovery

## Intent

Use one visible cause for hidden material becoming legible. Aim, lamp mouth, airborne marks and receiving region should agree. Choose this when a search, scan or reveal is the meaning, rather than adding a luminous decoration after the composition is finished.

## Compose and call

1. Establish legible dark contours and prepare a registered lit material with identical geometry.
2. Specify mount, emitterOffset and target in one coordinate space; solve once per absolute time.
3. Draw reveal using drawReceiverMask and beam using drawBeam; use coverageAt for motes.
4. Derive visible lamp position and direction from that same state. Include actor motion before solving.
5. Apply one camera to every consumer. The orbit fixture demonstrates uniform camera movement; archive holds the camera fixed.
6. Give the target purposeful holds/returns. The archive sinusoid is a technical sweep, not a claim of nuanced performance.

## Different content

Archive places warm side-light against six geometric tokens and a shelf. Orbit uses a cool overhead lamp, elliptical tracks, a central disk, nonuniform object sizes and moving camera. Their underlying drawings and material roles differ; neither contains reference artwork or a character copied from a film. Both are deliberately simple synthetic fixtures, not finished art-direction examples.

## Reject / use something else

For generic between-shot reveal, use iris-reveal or linear-mask. For luminous reflections, use light-echo-field. For physical ray refraction, use ray-chain within its contract; this field cannot substitute for it. Do not use an adjustable ellipse as proof of real wall illumination, shadows or photometric accuracy. Do not use this helper to claim material/detail quality it does not generate.

## Review prompts

Freeze reversals and check lamp front attachment. Move only the camera and check mask registration. Set intensity to zero and inspect no reveal. Check motes behind the origin and beyond target. Confirm the lit layer has intentional material differences rather than a white disk over the same image. Judge continuity/visual finish separately from numeric tests; silent technical fixtures have no listening evidence.
