## Static and raster limits

Neither recipe implements time, interaction, cloth physics,3D or accessibility certification. A future video adapter requires separate timeline and transformed-containment validation. Native placement inherits the host transform/clip/alpha/composite; arbitrary translated/expanded or PNG-decoded versus backing-canvas raster equality is not promised. Drape's per-tile pixel bound excludes encoder overhead and aggregate allocations. Radial label AABBs/actual ink are conservative and can reject rather than reflow. Matching radial/summary colors require shared tone/hueOffset.

Public publication and latest combined source-history checks are separate gates from standalone tests.
