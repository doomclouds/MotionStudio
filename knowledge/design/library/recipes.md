# 配方与候选书架

[返回总索引](INDEX.md)。先读[源码角色与分层](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/README.md)。下列只为大目录分区，不改包身份、不授予成熟度；名称相近也可能输入/权利完全不同。每次进入包的README后继续读其契约、设计指导、例子、manifest与许可，按本次内容分析选择。

## 既有组合入口

当需求是明确读序、证据编排、句法强调或单位/份额表达时先查此层；不要为了新颖直接跳进底层候选。

[UI总索引中的组合导航](../../../engine/design-elements/Index.md)保留完整入口；可按阅读目的进入[顺序焦点/编辑证据](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/reviewed/b06/README.md)、[句法/份额](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/reviewed/b08/README.md)、[强调词/单位图符](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/reviewed/b09/README.md)、[文本片段/数值格网](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/reviewed/b10/README.md)。`reviewed`目录不是用户审美批准。

## 文本、字符与阅读关系

当问题是段落角色、标题与注记的空间关系、字形证据或字符/评审片段的可读组织时查这里；进入原件核语言、字体、fit与溢出边界。

- [富文本段落](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/rich-paragraph-candidate/README.md)、[墨迹与注记区](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/ink-bay-candidate/README.md)、[字形证据](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/glyph-evidence-candidate/README.md)。
- [空间字符](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/spatial-character-snapshot-candidate/README.md)、[评审片段](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/review-tiles-candidate/README.md)、[静态标题效果](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/static-effects-candidate/README.md)。

## 数据、份额与同源证据

当多个视图需要共同解释同一份数值、总量、类别或作者给定的编辑关系时查这里；先核模型语义与图形编码是否相符，而非只选好看的图形。

- [指标板](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/metric-board/README.md)、[流量账本](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/flow-ledger-candidate/README.md)、[演示账本](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/invoice-demo-ledger/README.md)。
- [径向类别](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/radial-node-candidate/README.md)、[语义面积](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/xhs08/README.md)、[显式段与方形](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/explicit-geometry-candidates/README.md)。
- [字节场诊断](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/byte-field-diagnostic-candidate/README.md)、[作者指定字节编辑](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/authored-byte-edits-candidate/README.md)。

## 时间、窗口与多视图关系

当需要表达当前/选择状态、时间区间、共享游标或同一对象的不同采样视图时查这里；先区分静态证据模型与实际应用运行能力。

- [字幕区间 / Cue intervals](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/cue-interval-candidate/README.md)、[离散时序](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/timing-diagram-candidate/README.md)。
- [采样窗与指定对话](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/sampled-window-dialogue-candidate/README.md)、[共享游标](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/shared-trace-candidate/README.md)、[体积切片](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/linked-volume-candidate/README.md)。

静态时间模型不等于播放器或已完成声画集成；字幕例子也不授予转录、任意语言或自动排版能力。需要实际运动/音频时再分别读[运动](motion.md)与[声音](sound.md)。

## 材质、像素与空间层

当主视觉需要有规则的场/纹理、像素组织、材料形态或空间分界时查这里；对照原契约判断它是有限静态构造还是本次所需的动态机制。

- [垂坠材质](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/drape-material-candidate/README.md)、[互补分界](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/complementary-seam-candidate/README.md)、[文件格网](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/file-grid-candidate/README.md)、[特征堆栈](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/feature-stack-candidate/README.md)。

## 从来源组合进入

当需求接近已有整组来源的内容组织、但尚不能映射到一个窄机制时，从这些包的设计解释反推组合关系；不能直接套来源品牌或把来源作品当可自由复制素材。

[OpenDesign三案](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/open-design-three/Index.md)、[第二组选择性沉淀](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/open-design-static-three/README.md)、[第二组候选源码](../../../engine/design-elements/recipes/candidates/od-second-group-candidate/README.md)保留各自来源与边界。批次历史可从[原UI总索引](../../../engine/design-elements/Index.md)进入，不把批次和来源品牌当能力分类或许可。
